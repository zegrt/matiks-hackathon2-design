Dispatch v2

Two levels of big numbers. The top bar shows the system-wide figures the judges score (time to resolve, cost per resolved ticket). The ticket header shows the figures the reviewer needs for this one ticket (streak, cohort, confidence, cost, wait). Both use condensed instrument-style numerals, not scoreboard type, so it stays a tool, not a game. Costs are in ₹.
